# Problem 2

``` r
problem2_df =
  tibble(
    norm_samp = rnorm(10),
    log_vec = norm_samp > 0,
    char_vec = c("My", "Name", "Is", "Tiffany", "And", "This", "Is", "For", "Problem", "Two"),
    fac_vec = factor(c("Low", "Medium", "High", "Low", "Medium", "High", "Low", "Medium", "High", "Low"))
  )
```

The mean of `norm_samp` is 0.0664469.

The mean of `log_vec` is 0.5.

The mean of `char_vec` is NA.

The mean of `fac_vec` is NA.

I can take the mean of `norm_samp` and `log_vec`, but not `char_vec` and
`fac_vec` because R cannot translate those vectors into numeric values.

``` r
as.numeric(pull(problem2_df, log_vec))

as.numeric(pull(problem2_df, char_vec))

as.numeric(pull(problem2_df, fac_vec))
```

R was able to convert `log_vec` and `fac_vec` into numeric values, but
not `char_vec`. R mapped `TRUE` and `FALSE` values to `1` and `0` for
`log_vec`, and mapped each factor level to its underlying integer for
`fac_vec`. For `char_vec`, R was unable to translate strings into
numeric values.
